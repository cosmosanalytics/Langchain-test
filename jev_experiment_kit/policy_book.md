# Northwind Outfitters: Refund and Returns Policy (Support Agent Edition)

Northwind Outfitters is a fictional online retailer of outdoor gear. This policy is the single source of truth for support replies. Customer-support replies are checked against the 15 numbered rules below.

## 1. Return window
Customers may return a non-defective item within 30 days of the delivery date. After 30 days we cannot offer a return or refund for a non-defective item.

## 2. Evidence for defects
When a customer reports a defective or damaged item, the agent must ask for at least one photo showing the problem.

## 3. Condition of returned items
Non-defective items must be unused, unwashed and in their original packaging with tags attached. We do not accept returns of used or washed items. Defective items are exempt from this rule.

## 4. Final sale items
Items marked "Final Sale" or "Clearance" cannot be returned or refunded. The only exception is an item that arrives defective, which is handled under the defect rules.

## 5. Refund method
Refunds go back to the original payment method only. Agents must not offer cash, gift cards or any other payout method.

## 6. Processing time
A refund is issued 5 to 7 business days after we receive and inspect the returned item. Agents must not promise a refund sooner than 5 business days.

## 7. Shipping costs
Original shipping fees are not refunded, except when the item is defective or we sent the wrong item. A prepaid return label is provided only in those two cases. In every other case the customer pays return shipping.

## 8. Order number
Before processing any return or refund, the agent must ask for or confirm the order number.

## 9. Sensitive information
Agents must never ask a customer for a full card number, a card security code (CVV) or a password.

## 10. Large refunds
Refunds over 500 US dollars require supervisor approval. The agent must not approve them directly and must tell the customer that a supervisor will review the request.

## 11. Inspection
Every refund is subject to inspection of the returned item. Agents must not guarantee that a refund will be approved before inspection.

## 12. Respectful tone
Agents must not blame, accuse or criticize the customer, even when the customer made a mistake.

## 13. Warranty
Electronics carry a one-year limited warranty. No other product category has a warranty. Agents must not claim warranty coverage for non-electronics and must not promise coverage longer than one year.

## 14. Gift purchases
Refunds on gift purchases go only to the original purchaser. A gift recipient may receive store credit instead, never a cash refund.

## 15. Next step
Every reply must end by stating one clear next step for the customer.

## Notes for checking replies
- A rule that does not apply to the situation (for example, rule 4 when the item is not final sale) is satisfied by default.
- A reply complies with a rule if it follows it or the situation does not trigger it.
