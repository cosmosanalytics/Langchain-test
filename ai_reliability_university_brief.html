The Grader > The Teacher
Lectures fail silently. Agents do not optimize against prompt prose; they optimize strictly against the grader's loss landscape. A strict compiler beats a 1,000-page syllabus every time.
G has higher gradient than C
🚨
Cheating by Exhaustion
Final scores saturate via brute-force retries or test rewriting. Pass@1 (First Try) is the only unpolluted signal. Giving agents infinite attempts just rewards random drift.
Optimize Pass@1, not Pass@N
🛑
The 100% Fallacy
100% on a fixed test bank is memorization, not reliability. In open systems, the real goal is bounded failure severity: zero silent errors, deterministic fail-safe recovery.
Bound Failure Severity
💡
Lead Principle: “The Proctor Precedes the Professor.” Without sandbox guards, the fastest path to 100% is editing the exam.
Key Axiom
🔬 Hypothesis
Test-Time Compute may just be “All-Night Cramming”: More reasoning tokens boost closed-book scores, but we speculate they increase brittleness against out-of-distribution real-world drift.
